Thank you for stopping by! My name is Nathaly, and I work at the intersection of **orthopaedics and engineering**.

As an undergraduate, I dreamed of using my engineering knowledge to make a meaningful contribution to society. When I got to graduate school, that dream started taking shape as I was exposed to 3D printing, finite element modeling, biomechanics, and crashworthiness. In 2019, I envisioned building an open-access platform where we could assess and simulate the effects of medical devices and orthopaedic implants on different populations with parametric models of the human body. My motivation was simple: to bring the medical and engineering communities closer together while offering alternatives to animal and human subject testing. I was certainly a dreamer, but a dreamer with a vision and a specific project, which earned me the Rackham International Student Fellowship.

Fast forward to today: I am a Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow developing **data-driven digital twins of rotator cuff tendons**, combining full volume experimental measurements with computational modeling to predict injury progression and optimize repair strategies.

Wow! A lot has changed, but a lot has also remained the same. I now have the tools and knowledge to bring that early vision closer to reality. I am still a dreamer who believes that understanding how damage evolves can revolutionize injury management. And I still dream of working alongside orthopaedic surgeons, physicians, and patients to create a future where clinical treatments account for what makes each of us unique.
