I am excited to have been named an **Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow** at the University of Michigan's [Michigan Institute for Data & AI in Society (MIDAS)](https://midas.umich.edu/)! Mentored by Professors Vikram Gavini and Xun Huan, I am developing digital twins of rotator cuff tendons to optimize repair strategies.
